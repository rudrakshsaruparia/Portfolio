PROJECT 02

Deliverability Recovery: Inheriting a Burned Domain

Overview

The hardest problem I have worked on, and the least visible on a dashboard. Before I took the function over, an outbound agency had been sending on the company's domain for around six weeks. The targeting was wrong, the volume was high, and the reply file filled up with unsubscribe and remove me requests from exactly the people we most needed to reach later. By the time the engagement ended, the sending reputation was damaged and the recipient list had been trained to ignore us.

What was actually wrong

  • Cold volume sent from a domain that also carried business correspondence, so reputation damage was not contained
  • No separation between cold sending and the primary domain, meaning there was no clean asset to fall back to
  • Negative engagement signals accumulated at scale: low opens, low replies, manual spam and remove requests
  • Prospects inside the core ICP burned by irrelevant messaging, which cannot be undone by fixing infrastructure alone
  • No record of what had been sent to whom, so re-contact risked repeating the same mistake

What I did about it

  • Audited the full send history and documented the failures campaign by campaign, which supported the internal case for ending the engagement
  • Reconstructed a suppression list of every contact touched during that period so the same people were not re-approached cold
  • Separated cold outbound from the primary domain going forward, so business email is never exposed to cold sending reputation
  • Rebuilt the India campaign on tightened ICP filters rather than volume, cutting send size and raising relevance per contact
  • Ran Campaign 2 LinkedIn led to keep producing pipeline while email reputation recovered, rather than pausing outbound entirely
  • Monitor open, bounce, and reply behaviour weekly as recovery indicators rather than as vanity metrics
Key Result

Outbound kept producing meetings throughout the recovery period by shifting channel rather than stopping. The 21.5% open rate in Project 01, and the 16.4% on the second campaign, are the visible residue of this problem, and it is the reason I now treat sending infrastructure as the first decision in any outbound build rather than a setup task to be handled in week two.

Lead Handoff and CRM

Every qualified lead from the campaigns above was written into Salesforce with qualification notes and routed to the relevant sales rep, so nothing sat in an inbox. Working Together: Outbound Setup

I build outbound engines for early stage B2B companies that have no outbound motion yet, or one that has stopped working.

Who this is for

  • Post seed and Series A B2B SaaS, roughly $1M to $10M ARR, where the founder is still doing sales or there are one or two SDRs running without a system
  • Teams whose sending domain or previous agency setup has stopped producing replies, and who need someone who has actually cleaned one up
  • Companies selling to non technical buyers, where outbound is genuinely the right channel rather than the only one anyone thought of
  • Anyone about to hand cold sending to an agency without separating it from their primary domain first

What the setup includes

  • ICP definition with hard filters: industry, headcount, revenue, region, seniority
  • Persona mapping across the roles that touch the buying decision
  • Target account list and contact database, built, cleaned and validated before upload
  • Sending infrastructure: dedicated domains and mailboxes bought under your company name, set up with SPF, DKIM and DMARC, then warmed
  • Multi step sequence design across email and LinkedIn, written for one persona and one problem
  • Campaign built and launched in your sending tool, with tracking and reporting in place
  • Handover document and a walkthrough session so your team can run it without me

How it runs

Week 1, day 1
Infrastructure first

Sending domains bought and configured, mailboxes created, SPF, DKIM and DMARC set, warmup started before anything else happens. Warmup needs three weeks minimum to be worth having, so it starts on day one and runs in the background through everything below. Cold sending is kept off your primary domain permanently.

Week 1 to 2
ICP and list

Kickoff call, ICP defined and agreed, personas mapped, first account list built and reviewed with you before anything is sourced at volume. Small list, tight filters. Volume is the last lever, not the first.

Week 3
Copy and build

Sequences written and reviewed, campaign built in the sending tool, contacts loaded and checked for duplicates and bad records, tracking and reply routing configured. One persona, one problem, per sequence.

Week 4
Launch and handover

Launch at reduced volume on domains with a full warmup behind them, monitor deliverability daily, scale only once bounce and placement are clean. Handover document and training session at the end of the week.

I handle the buying and configuration of domains and mailboxes, but they are purchased under your company name and on your accounts, so you own them outright from day one. Tool subscriptions are billed to you directly. I scope the exact stack with you before anything is bought.

What I do not promise

I do not guarantee a number of meetings. Pipeline depends on your offer and your market as much as on the outreach. What I commit to is a system built properly, launched on clean infrastructure, and reported honestly every week so you can see exactly what is working.
